# Call5 Democracy v0.4.06 - Quick Update

**Released:** February 9, 2026

---

## What's New

### 🚀 One-Click Invitation Sharing
No more copy-pasting! When you're inviting friends to Call5 Democracy, just click "Load Template" and your personalized invitation message appears instantly. Edit if you want, or send as-is.

### 🔒 Privacy Enhanced
Your email address is no longer displayed at the top of every page. We now show your name instead - cleaner and more private.

### 👥 Cleaner Interface for Everyone
Admin tools are now hidden from regular users. You'll only see the features you can actually use - no more confusion about buttons that don't apply to you.

---

## Why These Changes?

**Feedback we heard:**
- "Copying and pasting the invitation text on my phone is annoying"
- "I don't like my email being visible all the time"
- "What's this Admin button for? I'm not an admin"

**What we did:**
- Made mobile sharing much easier
- Protected your privacy better
- Simplified the interface for everyone

---

## What Stays the Same

✓ All your existing features work exactly as before
✓ Your data is safe and unchanged
✓ Your invite link is still the same
✓ No new learning curve - it just works better

---

## Try It Out

1. **Log into Call5 Democracy** at voter-engagement-app.onrender.com
2. **Click "Share Your Invite Link"** from your dashboard
3. **Try the new "Load Template" button** in the email invitation form
4. **Notice the cleaner header** showing your name instead of email
